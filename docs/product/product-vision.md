# Product Vision

TechOps Hub is a multi-tenant SaaS operating system for solo and small tech-service businesses. It consolidates customer records, device context, service work, appointments, invoicing, and payment workflows into one focused application.

The product is intentionally designed for technicians and owner-operators rather than general CRM or enterprise ITSM use cases. Its defining capability is the customer tech profile, which captures the devices, network equipment, smart-home hardware, and service history required to support residential and small-business clients efficiently.
